# ADR-0017: No public social feed; invite-only groups only

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-28
- **Decided by:** Project owner, with Claude Opus 5 (session 60f2e23f)

## Context

An open social feed looks free and is not. Public posting plus stranger DMs in a body-image-adjacent category generates harassment, spam, illegal supplement sales and pro-eating-disorder content. Moderation is a human cost that scales with usage — violating ADR-0001 rules 2 and 4 — plus unbounded legal exposure.

## Decision

Invite-only private groups. No public feed, no discovery, no DMs from strangers. Shared programs and leaderboards within a closed graph only.

## Consequences

The accountability loop that makes Ladder effective is preserved, while the moderation cost stays at zero because the graph is closed. Cost: slower viral growth. Accepted deliberately.

## Alternatives considered

A public feed with automated moderation. Rejected — automated moderation of ED content is not reliable, and the failure mode harms vulnerable users.
